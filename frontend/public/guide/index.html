Nisaba user guide

Nisaba is a desktop inventory-sync manager: it keeps one canonical catalog of your products on your own machine and synchronizes quantities and listings with the marketplaces you sell on — eBay, Amazon, Squarespace, and XMR Bazaar. Everything runs locally. There is no cloud account, no telemetry, and no crash-reporting service.

Your keys, your machine

Nisaba uses your own marketplace API credentials on your own computer. There is no shared Nisaba keyset. That is why some platform steps — like the eBay account-deletion exemption — are things you do once in each platform's own developer portal, not in Nisaba.
